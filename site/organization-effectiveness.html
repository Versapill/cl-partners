Organization Effectiveness

Build the organization your strategy needs.

We help leaders align governance, decision rights, structure, roles and ways of working with the strategy. We work through the choices that affect resource allocation, cost, capacity and delivery, then support implementation with the people who will run the organization.

Discuss your situation

When leaders call us

The strategy is clear. The organization is not yet built to deliver it.

Growth has outpaced the organization: accountabilities overlap, processes vary or decisions are bottlenecked.

An acquisition needs integration, or a separation or carve-out needs a viable standalone organization, clear interfaces and continuity of operations.

Restructuring or simplification must reduce cost or complexity while protecting important capabilities.

Technology changes the work and raises questions about roles, processes and decision rights.

FocusGovernance, operating-model choices, clear interfaces and an implementation roadmap.

PE portfolio value creation

The organization behind the value-creation plan.

We work with operating partners, value-creation teams and portfolio-company leaders on the organization and execution needs behind a value-creation plan.

The mandate can focus on post-acquisition integration, management responsibilities, simplification or scaling. We clarify the respective roles of the investor, management team and CL Partners, and agree how progress will be assessed.

How we help

From diagnosis to an organization that keeps improving.

Diagnose

The business constraint: what needs to change, what is in the way and who owns the result.

Design

Governance, decision rights, structure, roles and processes, with the people who will run them.

Implement

A practical roadmap, accountable owners and milestones.

Sustain

Strengthen the team’s ability to keep improving after we step back.

In practice

What the business says

A business may initially describe the issue as “slow decision-making.”

What the diagnosis reveals

The real constraint is a combination of overlapping accountabilities, unclear decision rights and too many decisions escalating to the leadership team.

What the work becomes

Not simply redrawing the organization chart. Clarifying who owns which decisions, redesigning key interfaces, simplifying governance and helping leaders operate differently within the new model.

The impact

Fewer escalations, clearer ownership and faster decisions where they belong.

What progress can look like

Measured against the business priority, not activity.

Progress may mean clearer decisions, less duplication, better use of capacity or more reliable delivery. We agree measures with the client against the business priority, with a baseline, accountable owners and review points.
