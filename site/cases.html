Cases

Experience from the inside.

These examples draw on CL Partners mandates and Christian Lefèvre’s prior executive experience. They describe the business context and experience behind CL Partners. Client identities remain confidential.

How to read these cases: CL Partners mandate Founder’s prior executive experience
01
CL Partners mandateLife sciencesFrance

Post-acquisition integration and leadership team development

Context & challenge

Following an acquisition, a French biologics site’s leadership team needed to accelerate integration while clarifying governance, accountabilities and shared priorities.

Our role & approach

CL Partners conducted a leadership diagnostic and facilitated an offsite as part of a broader High Impact Leadership Team journey focused on integration, designed to strengthen alignment, ownership and effective collaboration over time.

02
Christian’s prior executive experiencePharmaceuticalsInternational

A complex corporate separation at scale

Context & challenge

A major pharmaceutical separation across an international manufacturing network, with corporate functions and manufacturing sites in scope.

Role & approach

Before founding CL Partners, Christian helped lead organization and workforce change during the separation. The work involved operating-model decisions, leadership alignment and business continuity.

Please note

This was prior in-house executive experience, not a CL Partners assignment. It is the foundation Christian brings to the firm’s work today.

03
CL Partners mandatePharmaceuticals

Change leadership through a multi-company integration

Context & challenge

A multi-company pharmaceutical integration required coordination across workstreams and leaders able to drive change beyond their functions.

Our role & approach

CL Partners supported change-leadership development for workstream leaders, focusing on integration priorities and engaging their teams, building the ownership needed to lead the integration themselves.

Facing a similar situation?

The same questions arise beyond life sciences: who decides, how the organization works and how leaders carry change into execution.
