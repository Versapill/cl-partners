What we look for

The qualities that make an Executive Business Skipper.

Business-minded

Starts with the result the client needs, not with a preferred methodology.

Experienced

Brings real operating or advisory experience and understands the complexity behind executive decisions.

Curious

Listens, explores and understands the context before jumping to a solution.

Direct and constructive

Comfortable saying what they see while building trust and maintaining respect.

Hands-on

Stays close to implementation and takes ownership for helping the work move forward.

Adaptive

Uses expertise and frameworks as tools, not prescriptions. The situation determines the approach.

Entrepreneurial

Comfortable with autonomy, ambiguity and building something together.

Human

Understands that sustainable performance depends on people, relationships, trust and leadership as much as on structure and process.
