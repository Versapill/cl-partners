What we look for

The qualities that make an Executive Business Skipper.

Expertise

Deep, practical experience you have earned leading or advising real organizations through change.

Strong values & integrity

You say what you see, respectfully and directly, and you keep your word with clients and colleagues.

Authenticity

You listen and adapt rather than deploy a standard toolkit, and clients trust you for it.

Self-authoring

You take ownership, set your own direction and bring others with you.

Curiosity

You want to understand the context before you recommend anything.

Growth & innovation

You are eager to grow, and to help build a partnership that grows with you.
