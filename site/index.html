Where we make a difference

When the business needs to move, but the organization makes execution harder.

Most of our work starts when the way a business is organized or led stands between its strategy and its results.

Organization Effectiveness Leadership & Team Effectiveness

  1. Growth outpaces the organization.

    Responsibilities overlap, processes vary and too many decisions sit with too few people.

  2. Transformation loses momentum.

    The direction is set, but governance, roles or ways of working slow delivery.

  3. M&A integration needs to turn a deal into a working business.

    Leaders need clear interfaces, with ownership of the integration priorities.

  4. A separation or carve-out needs a viable standalone organization.

    Clear interfaces and continuity of operations through the transition.

  5. A PE portfolio company needs to deliver its value-creation plan.

    Organization choices, management capacity and implementation ownership must support the investment priorities.

  6. A leadership team is not yet delivering as a team.

    Priorities compete, decisions take too long or ownership is unclear.

  7. A critical transition raises the bar.

    A new leader, remit or milestone calls for faster alignment.
