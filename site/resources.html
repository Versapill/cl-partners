Leading Teams

Behind the Mask

30 min Pairs + plenary Paper + pens
  1. Draw · 7 minOutside: what others see. Inside: a concern or need.
  2. Share · 10 minIn pairs, reveal only what you choose.
  3. Connect · 8 minShare useful themes in plenary, with permission.
  4. Agree · 5 minChoose one supportive team habit to try.

Reflect: What did we understand differently? What support would help?

Follow up: Review the habit together in two weeks.

Passing is welcome. Do not interpret another person’s drawing.
