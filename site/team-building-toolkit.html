About the toolkit

A guide to shared experiences.

Teams thrive when people build trust, enjoy being together and share positive energy.

A shared challenge creates a chance to combine strengths, experience success, notice reactions and learn together. Start with the experience your team needs, then choose an activity to match.

Connect & Discover · Curiosity & connection

Human Bingo 01

20 min 8-30 people Bingo sheets + pens

Discover colleagues beyond their roles.

  1. Prepare · 3 minExplain the prompts. Seek different names; allow repeats only if needed.
  2. Connect · 10 minCirculate and exchange a short story before adding a name. Passing is welcome.
  3. Share · 7 minEach person shares one discovery, with their colleague’s permission. Discuss what surprised you.

Reflect: What connection surprised you? Who would you like to know better?

Follow up: Continue one conversation during the offsite.

Use non-intrusive prompts. Offer seated small-group rotations.

When to use them

Moments when a shared experience helps.

A new or merged team needs to get to know one another quickly, beyond job titles.

An offsite opening that sets the tone for open, energetic work.

After a demanding period, to lift the energy and celebrate what the team achieved.

Before a cross-functional project, to practise coordination and shared leadership in a safe setting.
