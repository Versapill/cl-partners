What is inside each note

Built to be used, not filed.

Every Field Note follows the same simple structure, so you can pick one up in a spare ten minutes and put it to work the same day.

7 questions

The questions worth asking before you act, in the order they matter.

3 traps

The common mistakes leaders make in this moment, and how to avoid them.

1 short exercise

10 to 20 minutes, on your own or with your team.

A real example

A situation from international life sciences, showing the questions at work.

How leaders use them

Three ways to put a Field Note to work.

  1. Before a decision. Work through the seven questions on your own before you commit to a course of action.

  2. With your leadership team. Share the note ahead of a meeting and use the exercise to structure the conversation.

  3. In a one-to-one. Use a question or two to coach a direct report through a new role or a stalled commitment.
