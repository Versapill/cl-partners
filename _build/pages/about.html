Why CL Partners exists

A transformation is not complete when the new structure has been designed. It is complete when the organization and its leaders can operate effectively within it.

CL Partners is a Geneva-based advisory and implementation firm. Our Executive Business Skipper approach connects organization effectiveness, collective leadership and practical delivery.

Christian Lefèvre founded the firm after an executive career leading organization and people change in international life sciences. That experience anchors our credibility; the business challenge determines how we shape each mandate.

  1. EntrepreneurGrowth, resource choices and the practical demands of building a business.
  2. Life sciences executiveSenior roles at Pfizer/Wyeth, UCB and Novartis: acquisitions, integrations, reorganizations and a major separation.
  3. CL PartnersExecutive judgment and hands-on involvement for leaders facing consequential change.

“My background combines entrepreneurship with senior executive roles at Pfizer/Wyeth, UCB and Novartis. In international life sciences, I helped lead organization and people change through acquisitions, integrations and reorganizations from the executive side, including a major pharmaceutical separation across an international network.

Working inside these businesses taught me how closely strategy, organization and collective leadership depend on one another. A new structure needs clear decision rights and workable interfaces. Leaders resolve competing priorities and take ownership of implementation. I founded CL Partners to bring that executive judgment and hands-on involvement to leaders facing consequential business decisions.

Today, I help leaders work through integration, restructuring, growth and critical leadership transitions, and I bring this perspective to conversations with PE operating partners and portfolio-company leaders. Life sciences is my strongest experience anchor; the business challenge determines where I can contribute.

Being an Executive Business Skipper means working as an experienced peer: listening closely, testing assumptions, challenging choices and helping turn decisions into action. My work can include selected fractional leadership responsibilities.”
