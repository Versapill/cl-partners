Meet Christian

“I’m an Executive Business Skipper and the founder of CL Partners.”

Pfizer / WyethUCBNovartisEntrepreneur

I work alongside CEOs and executive teams on organization and leadership decisions behind growth, M&A integration and transformation.

My background combines entrepreneurship with senior executive roles at Pfizer/Wyeth, UCB and Novartis. In international life sciences, I helped lead organization and people change through acquisitions, integrations and reorganizations from the executive side. One of them was a major pharmaceutical separation across an international network. This was my prior in-house experience; it is the foundation I bring to my work today.

Working inside these businesses taught me how closely strategy, organization and collective leadership depend on one another. A new structure needs clear decision rights and workable interfaces. Leaders resolve competing priorities and take ownership of implementation. I founded CL Partners to bring that executive judgment and hands-on involvement to leaders facing consequential business decisions.

Today, I help leaders work through integration, restructuring, growth and critical leadership transitions. I also bring this perspective to conversations with PE operating partners and portfolio-company leaders about organization and execution needs behind a value-creation plan. Life sciences is my strongest experience anchor; the business challenge determines where I can contribute.

Organization effectiveness

Governance, structure, roles, processes and operating model.

Team effectiveness

Priorities, decisions, ownership, accountability and collective delivery.

I start with the business constraint, then work with the client on the combination required.

Being an Executive Business Skipper means working as an experienced peer: listening closely, testing assumptions, challenging choices and helping turn decisions into action. Within CL Partners, I work alongside the people responsible for delivery, with clear scope, decision rights and implementation responsibilities.

My work can include selected fractional leadership responsibilities. My entrepreneurial experience also shapes how I think about growth, resource choices and the practical demands of building a business.

If growth, integration or transformation is testing how your organization works, I’m happy to compare perspectives. Tell me what needs to change and what is getting in the way.
