CL Partners mandateLife sciencesFrance

Post-acquisition integration and leadership team development

Context & challenge

Following the acquisition of a French biologics site, the leadership team had to accelerate integration while continuing to run the business.

Beyond the formal integration plan, the team needed greater clarity around shared priorities, governance, accountabilities and how it would operate collectively through the transition.

Role & approach

CL Partners started with a leadership-team diagnostic and worked with the team to identify the organizational and leadership issues most likely to affect execution.

A pivotal offsite created alignment around the business context, team priorities and ways of working, as part of a broader High Impact Leadership Team journey. The work connected what the organization needed to deliver with how the leadership team needed to operate to make it happen.

Impact & progress

The Site Leadership Team was able to manage the integration without losing focus on a critical business milestone: getting the production site certification-ready on time. At the same time, clearer shared priorities, ownership and ways of working created a stronger foundation for the next phase of the integration.

Large-scale corporate separation

Context & challenge

A major pharmaceutical separation required organization and workforce change across approximately 10,000 employees and more than 20 manufacturing sites.

The challenge went far beyond separating structures. The new organization had to maintain business continuity while redefining operating models, accountabilities, capabilities and leadership arrangements at scale.

Role & approach

In his prior corporate executive role, Christian helped lead organization and workforce aspects of the separation.

The work required coordination across businesses, functions and geographies, translating separation strategy into practical organizational decisions while supporting leaders through significant uncertainty and change.

Impact & progress

The experience contributed to the successful establishment of the new organization while maintaining continuity through a complex international separation.

More importantly for CL Partners today, it shaped a core belief: a transformation is not complete when the new structure has been designed. It is complete when the organization and its leaders can operate effectively within it.

CL Partners mandatePharmaceuticalsEurope & MENA

Change leadership through a three-company integration

Context & challenge

Three pharmaceutical companies were being integrated, and substantial change-management material had already been produced.

The challenge was that the organization had change slide decks, but not yet consistent change leadership. Leaders across the three companies needed to understand the impact of the integration, make sense of the change themselves, and be able to explain it credibly to their teams.

Role & approach

CL Partners started with a change impact assessment, using interviews across the organization to understand where the integration was creating uncertainty, friction and practical concerns.

The findings were then used to design a change-leadership program starting with the most senior leaders and cascading through the organization. The focus was practical: leaders needed to be able to explain the why, what and implications of the change, believe in the direction themselves, and take ownership of supporting their teams through it.

Impact & progress

Leaders across the three companies developed a consistent and credible change story and took ownership of cascading the change through their teams. The process surfaced real integration concerns early enough to address them rather than allowing them to remain hidden.

As leadership visibility and ownership increased, employee engagement with the integration also strengthened across the three organizations.

04
CL Partners mandateCRO / Life sciencesEurope & US

From HR audit to interim transformation leadership

Context & challenge

A regional HR organization supporting Europe and the US had become fragmented, with inconsistent practices, unclear accountabilities and limited confidence in HR’s ability to support the business.

An initial HR audit showed that recommendations alone would not be enough; the organization needed experienced leadership to stabilize the function and move quickly into execution.

Role & approach

Following the audit, CL Partners moved from advisor to interim regional HR leadership, working directly with the business while driving a focused transformation agenda.

The mandate combined day-to-day leadership with structural improvements: clarifying the HR operating model and responsibilities, implementing a new HRIS, introducing a new performance process, developing a job-evaluation framework and strengthening the link between roles, skills and cost. The objective was not simply to improve HR processes, but to make HR a more effective enabler of business performance.

Impact & progress

The organization moved from fragmented HR practices toward a more consistent and performance-oriented model. Concrete outcomes included:

  • a new performance-management process;
  • implementation of a new HRIS;
  • a new job-evaluation framework;
  • clearer role and skill architecture;
  • stronger visibility on the cost of skills and capabilities;
  • better support for business decisions and new commercial opportunities requiring clear workforce and capability economics.

The work created a stronger platform for HR to support performance, while enabling the incoming permanent leadership team to continue the transformation from a more stable base.

Across these situations, the context changes (acquisition, separation, integration) but the underlying challenge is often similar: the organization can only deliver the change when structure, accountability and leadership move together.
