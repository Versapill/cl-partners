Our philosophy

Executive Business Skipper is our way of working: an experienced peer who understands where the business needs to go, listens to its leaders, challenges constructively, tests the choices and helps turn decisions into action.

In practice

Five behaviours you will recognise in the work.

A philosophy only matters if it changes what happens in the room, and after it.

  1. Start with the business result, not a standard method.

  2. Understand the system, not just the symptom.

  3. Say what we see, respectfully and directly.

  4. Work alongside the team through implementation, with agreed responsibilities and decision rights.

  5. Build the team’s ownership and capability so progress can continue without us.
