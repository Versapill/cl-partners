What makes a Perspective

Written from the executive side.

Executive Field Notes help a leader do something. Perspectives help a leader see a situation differently: explore a tension or challenge an assumption.

  • One specific business situation, not a general theory
  • What we have observed, and what leaders often misunderstand
  • Which decisions matter, and why
