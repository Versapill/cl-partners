Toolkit 01 · Team Growth

Team Effectiveness Conversations

A guide to growing together. Effective teams share a meaningful purpose, clear goals, roles and decisions. They build trust, speak openly, combine strengths and hold one another accountable for results.

High-impact teams go further: they challenge assumptions, adapt and create value beyond their own boundaries. This toolkit helps a team notice where it stands, choose what matters now and develop it together. Open to everyone, no sign-up.

    Human Bingo 01

    {{icon:clock}} 20 min{{icon:users}} 8-30 people{{icon:file}} Bingo sheets + pens

    Discover colleagues beyond their roles.

    1. {{icon:target}}
      Prepare · 3 minExplain the prompts. Seek different names; allow repeats only if needed.
    2. {{icon:message}}
      Connect · 10 minCirculate and exchange a short story before adding a name. Passing is welcome.
    3. {{icon:users}}
      Share · 7 minEach person shares one discovery, with their colleague’s permission. Discuss what surprised you.

    Reflect: What connection surprised you? Who would you like to know better?

    Follow up: Continue one conversation during the offsite.

    Use non-intrusive prompts. Offer seated small-group rotations.

    Set up · Guide · Close together

    Prepare and facilitate.

    Agree the purpose
    Agree the experience and positive energy you want. Give everyone a useful contribution.
    Size the teams and time
    Use the card’s team size and total time, including briefing, activity and debrief. Split larger groups.
    Prepare and brief
    Trial the setup. Explain the goal, rules and success criteria. Offer alternative roles.
    Guide the experience
    For competition, use equal conditions and clear scoring. Observe coordination, pressure and speed-quality trade-offs.
