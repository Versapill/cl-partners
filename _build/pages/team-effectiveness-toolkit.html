About the toolkit

A guide to growing together.

Effective teams share a meaningful purpose, clear goals, roles and decisions. They build trust, speak openly, combine strengths and hold one another accountable for results.

High-impact teams go further: they challenge assumptions, adapt and create value beyond their own boundaries. This toolkit helps a team notice where it stands, choose what matters now and develop it together. Open to everyone, no sign-up.

    Leading Teams · Trust & psychological safety

    Behind the Mask 16

    {{icon:clock}} 30 min{{icon:users}} Pairs + plenary{{icon:pencil}} Paper + pens

    Build understanding and trust.

    1. {{icon:pencil}}
      Draw · 7 minOutside: what others see. Inside: a concern or need.
    2. {{icon:message}}
      Share · 10 minIn pairs, reveal only what you choose.
    3. {{icon:link}}
      Connect · 8 minShare useful themes in plenary, with permission.
    4. {{icon:circle-check}}
      Agree · 5 minChoose one supportive team habit to try.

    Reflect: What did we understand differently? What support would help?

    Follow up: Review the habit together in two weeks.

    Passing is welcome. Do not interpret another person’s drawing.
