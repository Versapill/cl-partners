How leaders use them

Three ways to put a Field Note to work.

  1. Before a decision. Work through the seven questions on your own before you commit to a course of action.

  2. With your leadership team. Share the note ahead of a meeting and use the exercise to structure the conversation.

  3. In a one-to-one. Use a question or two to coach a direct report through a new role or a stalled commitment.
