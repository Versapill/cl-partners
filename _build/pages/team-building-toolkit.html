About the toolkit

A guide to shared experiences.

Teams thrive when people build trust, enjoy being together and share positive energy.

A shared challenge creates a chance to combine strengths, experience success, notice reactions and learn together. Start with the experience your team needs, then choose an activity to match.

Human Bingo 01

{{icon:clock}} 20 min{{icon:users}} 8-30 people{{icon:file}} Bingo sheets + pens

Discover colleagues beyond their roles.

  1. {{icon:target}}
    Prepare · 3 minExplain the prompts. Seek different names; allow repeats only if needed.
  2. {{icon:message}}
    Connect · 10 minCirculate and exchange a short story before adding a name. Passing is welcome.
  3. {{icon:users}}
    Share · 7 minEach person shares one discovery, with their colleague’s permission. Discuss what surprised you.

Reflect: What connection surprised you? Who would you like to know better?

Follow up: Continue one conversation during the offsite.

Use non-intrusive prompts. Offer seated small-group rotations.

Agree the purpose

Size the teams and time
